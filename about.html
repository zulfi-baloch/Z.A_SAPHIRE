A Little About Me

I'm Zulfiqar, a web developer who started learning web development because I wanted to understand how websites are actually made instead of just using them. I've spent around 3 years learning, experimenting and building different types of websites.

I'm still growing as a developer, so I'm not going to pretend I know everything. What I do care about is making websites that look clean, work properly, load well and give the person visiting them a good experience.

I enjoy working with modern layouts, responsive design, animations, visual effects and turning an idea into an actual website.
